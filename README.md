## Frameworks

### Porter's Five Forces
Analyze competitive intensity through:
1. Threat of new entrants
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Industry rivalry

### SWOT Analysis
Classic framework: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. Best used for positioning against specific competitors.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD)
Understand what job customers hire your product (and competitors' products) to do. Reveals competitive dynamics that feature comparisons miss.

### Competitive Positioning Map
Plot competitors on a 2x2 matrix using the two dimensions most important to your buyers. Identifies gaps and opportunities.

### Product & Pricing Intelligence
- [G2](https://www.g2.com/) — Software reviews and comparisons.
- [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/) — Software comparison platform.
- [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/) — B2B technology reviews.

### Market Data
- [Statista](https://www.statista.com/) — Market statistics and industry reports.
- [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/) — Industry market research reports.
- [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/) — Technology research and Magic Quadrants.

### Job Postings (Competitive Signal)

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/)
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/)
- [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/) — Compensation data across tech companies.
